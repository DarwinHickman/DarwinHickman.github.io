Hej, Hi, and welcome to my personal website.
I'm a plant ecologist, broadly-speaking, from the West Midlands of the UK but living in Uppsala, Sweden, and working for the Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences as a Researcher in Weed Ecology and Management.
From bachelor's and masters' work involving weed biocontrol insects and pests of forestry, I moved into a PhD in chemical interactions between cereals and the grass weed black-grass. As a postdoc, I broadened my horizons, building my interests in cropping systems, management, and plant community ecology, and I'm now developing a vision to integrate fundamental principles from plant ecology into weed ecology and management, including breaking apart the context-dependency of crop-weed interactions. 
Please reach out if you think that anything of this is exciting!
